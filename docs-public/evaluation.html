Primary and secondary metrics

The primary metric is Hit@±1: the share of predictions whose absolute error is at most one. Supporting metrics include position-level Hit@±1, all-position Hit@±1, MAE, MSE, and RMSE.

Baselines

Model results are compared under the same chronological conditions with Random, fixed value, mean, median, last value, frequency, and an approved statistical baseline.

Evaluation discipline

  • Train, Validation, Holdout, and Prospective are separated in time order.
  • Scalers, encoders, feature selection, and hyperparameter tuning fit only within Train.
  • Multiple seeds retain mean, variance, and worst-seed results.
  • Prospective predictions are sealed before corresponding actual values are known.
